Create Email Acount

Learn how to create a email account so we can contact you.


Create a Gmail (Google) Account (Recommended)

  1. Go to the Google Accounts site and click Create account.
  2. Click Personal use.
  3. Enter your birth date and gender.
  4. Pick a Gmail address username or create your own custom name.
  5. Make a strong password with letters and numbers.
  6. Add your phone number to verify the account with a text code (optional but helpful).
  7. Read and agree to the privacy rules and terms.
  8. And enjoy your new email acount



Create an Outlook (Microsoft) Account

  1. Go to the Outlook site and click Create free account.
  2. Pick a username for your new email address.
  3. Make a strong password with letters and numbers.
  4. Enter your first and last name.
  5. Select your country/region and birth date.
  6. Type the characters you see on the screen to prove you're not a robot.
  7. And enjoy your new email acount



    8. Create a Yahoo! Account

    1. Go to the Yahoo! Acount Creation site
    2. Enter your first and last name.
    3. Pick a username for your new email address.
    4. Make a strong password with letters and numbers.
    5. Enter your mobile phone number, birth date, and gender.
    6. Click Continue.
    7. Yahoo! will send a verification code to your mobile phone. Enter the code to verify your account.
    8. And enjoy your new email acount
