Turn On Dark Mode


Chrome

First, go to Google Chrome Settings. Then, click Appearance. Finaly, change the mode to dark.


Applr Safari (Mac)

First, click the Apple logo in the top left corner of your screen. Then, open System Settings (or System Preferences). Then, click Appearance (or General) on the side menu. Finaly, select Dark at the top to change your system theme to dark mode.


Apple Safari (iPhone)

First, open the Settings app. Then, tap Display & Brightness. Finaly, select Dark under the Appearance section. Alternatively, open your Control Center, touch and hold the brightness slider, and tap Dark Mode.


Microsoft Edge

First, click the three dots (Settings and more) icon in the top-right corner. Then, select Settings from the drop-down menu. Then, click on Appearance on the menu on the left. Finaly, find Overall appearance and pick Dark.


Mozilla Firefox:

First, click the three lines (Applications Menu) in the top-right corner. Then, click Settings. Then, click Appearance from the menu on the left. Finaly, under Website appearance, click Dark.


Opera

First, click the Easy Setup icon (three horizontal sliders) in the top-right corner. Then, click Themes (or Appearance) on the menu on the left. Finaly, Select the Dark option.
